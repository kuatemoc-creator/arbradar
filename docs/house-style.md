# ArbRadar house style

Derived from `docs/style-corpus.md`. The rules the build applies on its own are
marked (rule); the rest guide the editorial model when it is switched on.

**Structure.** One typeface, one headline size, one text size. Sections in this
order: Today's headlines; Also (held over or brief); From the ICSID docket;
Filings; In the courts; People. Nothing gets a label, a tag or an eyebrow. (rule)

**Headlines.** Party, verb, object. Sentence case; only names keep capitals.
Present tense. Six to twelve words. No colon, no "BREAKING", no outlet name, no
question. Name the State and the kind of party ("Chinese steelmaker",
"Malaysian airline", "Libyan state entity"), the forum by its acronym, the
instrument when it is the point (ECT, NAFTA, BIT). Verbs from the corpus: wins,
loses, defeats, seeks, threatens, launches, files, lodges, enforces, pays,
settles, fails to, faces, upholds, annuls, sides with, orders, joins, leaves,
names, instructs. (rule for case and prefixes; the model rewrites the rest)

**Explanations.** One sentence, 25–45 words, never cut mid-sentence. Subject by
nationality and kind or the State or the court; then the act, forum, amount
with currency, and one clause of context. "Says it has won" and "reportedly"
where the claim is the party's own. No "significant", no "landmark", no "why it
matters". (rule for whole sentences; the model writes the sentence)

**Sources.** The outlet name and date after the explanation, as a link. Prefer
the wire or the trade press copy. (rule)

**People.** Moves and appointments are headlines like any other when they come
from the trade press; the People list carries the rest. (rule)
